Time-of-flight ball tracking

Ball-height control on an Arduino Due running uC/OS-II. A time-of-flight sensor measures where the ball is, and a PID loop sets the fan speed to hold it at the requested height.

Structure

Each job runs as its own uC/OS-II task: reading the sensor, reading the setpoint, updating the display, and driving the fan. Tasks pass data through message queues, and semaphores protect the hardware they share.

What I changed

I raised the sensing and control update rate from 5 Hz to 20 Hz, which gives the PID loop four times as many corrections per second, and tuned the gains so the ball responds better when the requested height changes.

Bringing the hardware up took some debugging too. Oscilloscope measurements and circuit checks helped me find wiring, pin-mapping, and PCB faults.
